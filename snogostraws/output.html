Party Pack Essentials

The OG SNOGO and INCOGNITO are premium, patented cacao accessories crafted from triple mirror-polished 316 stainless steel. These low-profile bump straws feature smooth, frictionless action for discreet cacao enjoyment. Key features include an internal DNX spring for silent recoil and a 7-hole pin-prick array for optimal airflow. They can be disassembled into a straw and scoop.

The Carry-N-Clean kit complements the straws, combining a Gunk Goblin cleaning brush and pick with a Litchi Italian leather keychain carrier. This stylish storage solution offers secure portability with an ergonomic carabiner-style keyring and snap-closure pocket.

Available in multiple finishes, these accessories blend functionality with luxury, catering to those who appreciate quality and discretion in their cacao consumption.

All4One

The All4One Bundle includes one of each type of Snogo Straw: INCOGNITO, SNOGO, MOJO, SMG, and SMG MOJO, along with two thick necklace chains. Available in Silver, Nitride, Burgundy, and Gold finishes, this bundle offers a 20% discount off the list price. Perfect for any occasion, it allows you to enjoy a complete set of stylish and versatile straws. 

Deca Bundle

Get 30% off the list price when you purchase 5 to 10 straws of the same type and color, including SNOGOs, INCOGNITOs, and SNOGO MOJOs. This offer is available through the Deca bundle, allowing you to choose your straw type, color, and quantity. Available colors include Silver, Nitride, Burgundy, Gold, and Rose Gold. Simply select your straw and quantity to take advantage of this discount.

 

Fiesta Uno

The Fiesta Uno bundle is an ideal gift set for various celebrations, including weddings, festivals, and special friendships. It allows you to choose between an engraved SNOGO or INCOGNITO straw, along with a matching necklace in either thick or thin styles. Available finishes include Silver, Nitride, Burgundy, and Gold, with sets ranging from 3 to 9 straws. Each set includes a Snogo QuickDraw and a Snogo Double Stack Large, making it a cherished keepsake. Customers can specify engraving details during checkout to personalize their straws.

Fiesta Dos

The Fiesta Dos bundle is a versatile gift set ideal for various celebrations, such as weddings and festivals. It allows customers to choose between a SNOGO or INCOGNITO straw, available in Silver, Nitride, Burgundy, or Gold finishes, along with a Fisherman Hat in either White or Black. Sets can be ordered in quantities of 3 to 9. Customers can personalize their straws with engraving (14 characters for SNOGO, 11 for INCOGNITO) during checkout. Each set includes an engraved straw, a matching Fisherman Hat, a Snogo QuickDraw, and a Snogo Double Stack Large.

The Rodeo

Because it’s not your first rodeo. You’ve ridden more than enough bulls; you’re a professional, and you deserve professional gear. That’s why you choose the Rodeo bundle, so you can party like the professional you are. The Rodeo gets you one 3-inch Snogo straw, in any finish, a thick necklace in the same finish, and a 4-pack of vials.

The Minx

Sexy and seductive, the Minx is never without company. Pick your finish –silver, gold, burgundy or nitride—and receive the coupling; both the 3-inch Sogo straw and the 1.5-inch incognito straw, a thick necklace and the thinner necklace  (in the same finish),  plus a 4-pack of vials.

The Hot Fox

Knowing what you want, that’s hot. So the Hot Fox begins by asking you to decide: silver & nitride or gold & burgundy?

After that, it gets real. Because with the Hot Fox bundle you get you a 3-inch Snogo straw and a 1.5-inch incognito straw in BOTH silver & nitride OR BOTH in gold & burgundy as well as the thick and thin necklaces in both finishes. So it’s a total of 4 straws and 4 necklaces, plus a 4-pack of vials.

The Vixen Viceroy

The most bountiful of bundles, the Vixen Viceroy is the fat of the land. The full Monty! It’s the 3-inch Snogo straw in all 4 finishes, the 1.5-inch Incognito straw in all 4 finishes, the thin and the thick necklaces, both, in all four finishes, the rhinestone baseball cap in all three colors, plus an 8-pack of vials.  It’s damn near everything! 8-straws, 8-necklaces, 3-baseball caps, 8-blends, and 8-vials.
